02

Timing spine

Every cut point is derived, not eyeballed. The track was transcribed, beat-tracked and locked to one formula — so a panel boundary can never drift off the music.

Bar formula

bar(n) = −0.0734 + n × 2.55021 s

  • 94.11 BPM, 4/4 — bar 2.55021 s, beat 0.63755 s
  • 79 bars, origin offset −0.0734 s
  • Panels are whole-bar or half-bar; nothing lands off-grid

The pipeline is not the problem. Both driver bugs that were burning capacity are fixed and proven in real spends: clips were coming back portrait (now locked 16:9), and the duration flag was parsed but never applied, so shots rendered at 8 s when the assembler only keeps 4–6 — roughly double the cost per shot for footage that was thrown away. The batch runner also now stops after a single walled job instead of grinding through six.

05

What is blocking the remaining shots

Nothing here is a code fault. Every generation lane is either out of allowance or ruled out on quality. This is the only thing standing between the current cut and a finished video.

Walled

Google Flow — primary lane

Produced 6 clips in a 12-minute burst, then the Create button disappeared entirely — that absence is the credit wall. Re-probed 8 hours later at the cheapest 4-second setting: still gone. The pool is empty, not the config.

Walled

Artlist Seedance — the "unlimited" lane

Unlimited now reads disabled, not merely unchecked, and the button has become "Get More Credits". The allowance is spent — signing in again would change nothing.

Walled

Higgsfield

All credits used; the driver port is down. Not a route to 49 shots.

Ruled out

LTX-Video

Free and unmetered, but the motion is visibly weaker and it would carry 49 of 64 shots. Called off — the look is not worth the throughput.

Refill throughput is the whole remaining question. At the observed Flow burst rate the 49 outstanding shots are roughly eight more refill cycles. Resuming is one command and it auto-skips everything already on disk, so no capacity is ever spent twice.
06

Open decisions

1 · Pick the Architect face

Free, unblocks 16 panels' worth of re-plating.

2 · Credit top-up, or wait out the refills

Paid spend has never been authorised on this project. Waiting is free; it just takes cycles.

3 · Nothing else is waiting on input

Every other stage is either finished or purely downstream of shot generation.
