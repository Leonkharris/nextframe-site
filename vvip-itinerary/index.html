How the night runs

A staged evening is a running order, not a guest list. This is the shape of it — every roster agreed with you and confirmed in writing before a single booking is made.

  1. 6:00HandoverVilla is ours. Sound, lighting and bar go in while the afternoon is still yours.
  2. 8:00ChefPrivate chef serves. Menu agreed in advance, paced to the room rather than the clock.
  3. 9:30Floor opensHosts and floor staff on. Full bar, music down low, nobody rushing anyone.
  4. 11:00The setBooked act — cabaret, pole or fire on the beach. Twenty minutes, then the room is loose again.
  5. 12:30DJProduction rig up and the night runs on its own. Close protection stays until the last guest leaves.

Moving — every leg, door to door

Every duration above is door to door and assumes the day of week it actually falls on. Where a leg has a range, the plan is built on the slower end — a schedule that only works in light traffic is not a schedule.
