How the night runs

Nothing about this night happens in a venue that also has other customers in it. The room is wherever you are staying; everything else — the rig, the bar, the chef, the company — comes to you. This is the shape of it, and every name on it is agreed with you first.

  1. 6:00The gear arrivesSound, lighting and bar go in while the afternoon is still yours. You are not there for the setup.
  2. 8:00ChefPrivate chef serves. Menu agreed in advance, paced to the room rather than the clock.
  3. 9:30Floor opensHosts and floor staff on. Full bar, music down low, nobody rushing anyone.
  4. 11:00The setBooked act — cabaret, pole or fire on the beach. Twenty minutes, then the room is loose again.
  5. 12:30No last ordersThe rig is ours for the night, so nothing closes and nobody is asked to leave. Close protection stays until you are done.
