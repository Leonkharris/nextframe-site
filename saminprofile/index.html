Who she is

The colorist behind the account

Samin learned her craft as a session stylist in New York, backstage at Fashion Week with the glam service Priv, and in Manhattan salons. In 2020 she moved her chair to Miami: Avant-Garde in Coral Gables, Danny Jelaca's salon, then Atma Beauty in Sunset Harbour.

Her specialty is blonde that looks lived-in rather than processed, and the corrections that take brassy orange back to cool.

Babylights · balayage · icy, buttery and golden blondes · colour correction · cuts · tape-ins · runway
