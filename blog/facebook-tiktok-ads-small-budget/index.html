Facebook and TikTok Ads on a Small Budget with AI

By Leon Harris, Founder & AI Creative Director · Updated August 2026

You boosted a post last week, it collected forty reactions, and not one person messaged you about the unit, the table, or the order. What you want is for every peso you spend to end in a Messenger thread with a real buyer. This guide shows you how to run Facebook and TikTok ads properly on a small daily budget, using free AI tools for the parts that usually cost you an agency.

Boost Post is not a campaign

Meta says it plainly: a boosted post "may initially optimize for Page likes, comments and shares or overall brand awareness," while ads built in Ads Manager "can optimize for app installs, website conversions, video views, shop orders and more." Boosting limits you to basic Feed placements and simple age, gender and interest targeting; Ads Manager opens up Messenger and Stories placements, lookalike audiences (people who resemble your existing customers), and objectives built for leads or sales rather than applause. TikTok has the same split. Its in-app Promote button is described on TikTok's own help page as "a full-funnel, lightweight advertising tool found within the TikTok app" — fine for a quick nudge, but TikTok Ads Manager is where you get real targeting, Spark Ads (turning one of your existing organic posts into a paid ad), and conversion tracking that ties spend to orders. If you have only ever tapped Boost or Promote, you have paid to widen a spotlight, not run a targeted campaign.

Pick one objective that matches money, not attention

Meta's campaign objectives are exactly six: Awareness, Traffic, Engagement, Leads, App promotion, Sales. If customers message you on Messenger, choose Engagement set to optimise for Messages, or Leads. If you sell through a website or a checkout, choose Sales. TikTok groups its objectives the same way — Awareness (Reach), Consideration (Traffic, Video views, Community interaction), and Conversion (App promotion, Lead generation, Sales). Chase reach or engagement for its own sake and the system will deliver exactly that: views and reactions, no customers.

Let AI write and stress-test your copy in your customers' own words

A general AI chat tool — ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini, all of which have free tiers — is genuinely useful here. Write a one-paragraph brief: what you sell, the price, the current promo, who it is for, and the single outcome you want. Then paste in ten to twenty real messages from your Messenger inbox, the actual questions and objections in your buyers' own wording, Taglish and all. Ask the AI to draft five to ten short, medium and long ad copy variants built from that real vocabulary, not generic marketing language. Then ask it to role-play a sceptical buyer and poke holes in each variant before you put money behind it. The value is not prettier sentences — it is ten angles to test in twenty minutes.

Structure a small test so it can tell you something

The instinct with a small daily budget is to spread it across many audiences. Do not. Concentrate the budget into two or three ad sets sharing your best copy, not six starved ones. A new ad set goes through what Meta calls the learning phase, a stretch where delivery is unstable and the early numbers do not mean much. Editing the budget, audience, creative or optimisation event restarts that process, so once a test is running, leave it alone for a few days. Change only one variable per round — a new audience or a new headline, never both — so you know what actually moved the result.

Reading your results table without guessing

Know what the columns mean before you judge. CPM is the cost per 1,000 times your ad is shown. CPC is the cost per link click. Frequency is impressions divided by reach — how many times the same person has seen your ad on average; a frequency that keeps climbing on a small audience means you are wearing out the same people. The number that matters most is cost per result: total spend divided by however many messages, leads or purchases your objective actually produced. If the table overwhelms you, export it, paste it into an AI chat tool, and ask it to rank your ad sets by cost per result and flag any set with rising frequency.

What AI cannot fix, and the PH practicalities

No AI tool can repair a weak offer, a two-day reply time to a Messenger lead, or a landing page that will not load on mobile data. Targeting and copy buy you the click; everything after the click is on you. Check those three before you scale a winner.

On payment, TikTok's Asia Pacific help page lists Bankcards, PayPal, Maya and GCash for Philippine advertisers on manual pre-pay in pesos, while automatic payment supports only bankcards and PayPal. On Meta, check what your own Ads Manager settings offer you — cards, PayPal and local manual payment methods vary by country. Set your ad account to Philippines and PHP first, since both platforms tie the options to that. One trap: Meta documents an Apple service fee when you add ad funds inside the Facebook or Instagram iOS app, which you avoid by paying from a desktop or mobile browser instead. And every ad, at any budget, is bound by Meta's Advertising Standards and TikTok's Advertising Policies — read the rules for your category before you scale spend.

Frequently asked questions

Is boosting a Facebook post the same as running a Facebook ad?

No. Meta's help centre distinguishes them: a boosted post "may initially optimize for Page likes, comments and shares or overall brand awareness," while ads built in Ads Manager "can optimize for app installs, website conversions, video views, shop orders and more." Placements, targeting and full reporting live in Ads Manager. If your goal is messages, leads or sales rather than visibility, build the ad there, not with the Boost button.

Which ad objective should a small business in the Philippines pick?

Match the objective to the outcome you actually need. Meta's six objectives are Awareness, Traffic, Engagement, Leads, App promotion and Sales. A Messenger-driven shop should use Engagement optimised for Messages, or Leads. A business with a website or checkout should use Sales. On TikTok the equivalent conversion objectives are Lead generation and Sales.

Can I pay for Facebook or TikTok ads in the Philippines with GCash or Maya?

On TikTok, its Asia Pacific payment page lists Bankcards, PayPal, Maya and GCash for the Philippines on manual pre-pay in pesos, while automatic payment supports only bankcards and PayPal. On Meta, cards, PayPal and country-specific local manual payment methods are offered, so check the exact options inside your own Ads Manager payment settings. Set your account country and currency to Philippines and PHP first.

How small a budget can I actually test with?

There is no official minimum figure worth quoting here. What matters is structure: concentrate your daily budget into two or three ad sets instead of splitting it across many, let each one get through its unstable early delivery period without edits before you judge it, and change only one variable per test round.

What do CPM, CPC and cost per result actually mean?

CPM is the cost per 1,000 times your ad is shown. CPC is the cost per link click. Frequency is impressions divided by reach, meaning how many times the same person has seen your ad on average. Cost per result is total spend divided by the number of messages, leads or purchases your chosen objective produced. That last one is the number that ties spend to a real customer.

Can AI actually write my ad copy for me?

A free AI chat tool can draft and stress-test copy variants well if you feed it a real offer brief plus your customers' actual Messenger wording, and it can help you read a results table. What it cannot do is fix a weak offer, a slow reply time to leads, or a page that will not load on mobile data. Those will sink your cost per result no matter how good the copy is.
