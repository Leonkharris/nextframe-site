Is Webild Free? What the AI Website Builder Really Offers Philippine Small Businesses

By Leon Harris, Founder & AI Creative Director · Updated November 2026

You saw Webild in a reel, you want a proper website for your business, and you do not want to hand over a card before you know what you are paying for. Picture a clean site that loads on your customers' phones and sends their enquiries straight to your Messenger or Viber. In the next few minutes you will know whether Webild is free, what it costs, and how to test it without wasting money.

Is Webild free?

Not according to its own pricing page. We checked webild.io/pricing on 18 November 2026, and it lists three paid plans only: Starter, Pro and Business. No free plan is shown. The name is Webild, not WeBuild, and the address is webild.io. Next-Frame has no affiliation with Webild, so this is a plain reading of what the company publishes, not a sales pitch. Pricing pages change, so screenshot the page on the day you decide.

What the plans cost and include

The page shows $24.99 for Starter, $49.99 for Pro and $99.99 for Business, each marked per month. It uses the dollar sign without naming the currency. A Monthly/Annually toggle carries an 80% OFF badge but shows no annual price, so confirm the real amount at checkout. Your bank adds its own exchange rate and fees, so we are not converting to pesos. Starter lists publishing, AI editing, a free domain or your own, hosting, SEO, removal of the Built with Webild badge, AI images and contact forms. Pro adds faster AI, premium templates, 3D mode, website analytics and priority support. Business adds unlimited websites, the fastest queue, personal support, and website transfer and code export.

Usage is metered in credits: 150 a month on Starter, 350 on Pro and 800 on Business, plus bonus credits in month one (100, 300 and 500). Per Webild's Terms of Service, credits do not roll over and used credits are non-refundable.

The free-tier contradiction

Webild's Terms say the Built with Webild badge must be shown on free tier sites, yet the pricing page shows no free tier. Some free preview may exist, but it is not advertised, so do not plan around it. The 14-day money-back guarantee is not a free trial either. It covers your first payment only, and only if you have not edited a website, published it, connected a domain, exported it, or used more than 10% of your plan's credits. In practice, building a real site cancels it.

The Terms name the company as Webild Inc., registered in Delaware, USA, with contact admin@webild.io. Sites are hosted through Vercel, a large web hosting company, and you keep ownership of the content you create. Taking your site elsewhere through export or transfer is listed only under Business.

What you really pay

The plan is not the whole bill. Starter says a free domain is included, but the pages we checked do not state for how long or what renewal costs, and the Terms say domain purchases are final and non-refundable. Add a business email, real photos of your shop, rooms, food or products, and the credits spent on later edits. Also not stated on the pages we checked: what web address a free site gets, how a Facebook, Messenger or Viber button is added, and any Philippine pricing. Ask before you pay.

A 20-minute test before you pay

Write ten lines about your business first: name, town or barangay, services with real peso prices, opening hours, your Facebook Page link, your Viber or Messenger contact, and pick five real photos. Then:

  • Minutes 0-5: if Webild offers a free preview, paste your description and generate a home page. If it does not, run the same test on another builder's free plan.
  • Minutes 5-10: open the result on your own phone, on mobile data. Is the text readable and are the buttons easy to tap with a thumb?
  • Minutes 10-15: tap every contact link: Messenger or Facebook, Viber, call button and form. Confirm a message actually reaches you.
  • Minutes 15-18: read every word. Fix wrong prices, invented services and generic stock photos.
  • Minutes 18-20: check whether the Built with Webild badge shows, and remember that leaving with your site is listed only under Business.

Do not edit, publish or connect a domain on a paid plan until you are sure, because that ends your refund. Before paying, email admin@webild.io and ask: is there a free tier or preview, what address does a free site get, what does the domain cost to renew, and can I take my content with me on Starter?

When something else is the better answer

If you mainly need to be found and messaged, start with a free Facebook Page and a free Google Business Profile, which puts you on Google Search and Maps. If you want a website with a free plan, Durable lists a $0 plan with a durable.site address and 300 AI credits in total, and Wix lists a free plan with no credit card required, though its free domain offer applies to paid plans only. Check each on its official page. For a wider comparison, see our guide to AI website builders for small business.

Honest limits

An AI-built site still needs real photos, correct prices and a human read-through. Showing up on Google takes more than a plan feature called SEO. Treat Webild as one option to test, not a promise.

Frequently asked questions

Is Webild free?

Not according to its own pricing page, checked 18 November 2026. It lists only Starter, Pro and Business, with no free plan. The Terms mention a badge rule for free tier sites, so some free access may exist, but it is not advertised. Confirm with Webild before building anything you care about.

Is webild.io free to try?

The pricing page offers a 14-day money-back guarantee, not a free trial. Per the Terms it covers the first payment only, and is lost once you edit, publish, connect a domain, export, or use more than 10% of your plan's credits.

How much does Webild cost in pesos?

Webild shows $24.99, $49.99 and $99.99 per month and does not name the currency or show peso prices. Your bank applies its own exchange rate and fees, so the peso amount is not fixed. Check your card statement after the first charge.

Can I connect my own domain and remove the Webild badge?

Starter lists a free domain or connecting your own, plus removal of the Built with Webild badge. How long the free domain lasts and what it costs to renew are not stated on the pages we checked. The Terms say domain purchases are final and non-refundable.

Do I own my website and can I take it elsewhere?

The Terms say you keep ownership of the content you create. Website transfer and code export are listed only on the Business plan ($99.99 a month as displayed). Ask Webild what you can take with you on a cheaper plan before you pay.

Is Webild the same as WeBuild?

We found no link. Webild (webild.io) is run by Webild Inc., a Delaware company according to its Terms. Reels sometimes mishear the name, so type the exact address webild.io and be careful with look-alike sites.
