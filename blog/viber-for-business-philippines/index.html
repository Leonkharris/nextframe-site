Viber for Business in the Philippines: Channels, Communities and AI-Drafted Broadcasts

By Leon Harris, Founder & AI Creative Director · Updated October 2026

Your customers are already on Viber, but your shop probably treats it as a personal chat app, or you started a group that went quiet or filled with spam. Imagine a steady, low-cost line to your regulars that brings them back for promos, restocks and bookings without paying for every post. This guide shows which Viber tool fits which job, how to set it up in minutes, and how to let AI draft a month of messages while you keep the final say.

Why Viber is worth the effort

Per Rakuten Viber executives quoted by Philstar (19 February 2025), the Philippines is among Viber's top five user countries out of 190, monthly active users in the country rose 21%, and the number of users joining channels and communities rose 17%. Those are Viber's own claims, so treat them as direction, not proof. They still suggest that many of your customers are already there.

What Viber actually offers a business

There are three different things, and mixing them up is why many shop groups fail. Channels are one-way: only admins post, and followers read, react and, if you enable it, comment. Per Viber's own blog, channels have invite links, keyword search, content scheduling and analytics, and they are free to create. Communities are two-way: members can chat, phone numbers stay hidden, and new members can see earlier messages. Admins can delete messages and remove members. Viber's help center compares the two.

The third is the official business route. In October 2023 Rakuten Viber launched free, self-serve Business Accounts for Philippine MSMEs, with a public profile, a product catalog and a business inbox kept apart from personal chats, according to BusinessWorld. Features have changed since, so check what your app shows today. Official business messaging, meaning promotional, transactional and conversational messages, is paid. It is priced per country and per message type and usually goes through an approved provider such as 8x8. We found no published Philippine price, so ask a provider for a quote.

Which one for which job

  • Channel: weekly specials, restock alerts, new menu or listing announcements.
  • Community: a suki group of regulars or VIP buyers who ask questions and swap tips. It needs a moderator.
  • Business messaging: booking confirmations and official notices, only if you can justify a paid partner.

Most small shops should start with one Channel. It is free, it is the easiest to keep clean, and nobody can fill it with their own ads.

Set up a Channel in ten minutes

  • Open Viber, tap Chats, tap Compose, then New channel, then Create channel. Menu names can differ slightly between app versions.
  • Add a name, an icon and a short description that says what people get and how often, for example "Weekly specials from [Shop], 1-2 posts a week."
  • Copy the invite link and save the QR code so you can print it.
  • If you add staff as admins, remember that the creator is the superadmin, and admins can use an alias to hide their personal profile (Viber admin guide).

Get customers to join without spamming

Ask, never add. Put the QR code at the counter and on receipts, share the link on your Facebook page, and drop it into your Messenger auto-replies, and always say what people will receive. Do not add customers by phone number without their say-so. The Data Privacy Act (RA 10173) centers on freely given, specific and informed consent, and gives people the right to object to direct marketing; the National Privacy Commission explains it at privacy.gov.ph, and the full law is on Lawphil. This is general information, not legal advice, so check with the NPC or a lawyer.

Let AI draft a month, then check by hand

Give your AI tool your shop name, your tone, the real facts, and four post types: promo, restock, booking reminder, and a tip or story. Ask for eight to ten posts of two or three short lines in the language your customers use, whether English, Tagalog or Taglish. The part that matters is the human check. Before anything goes out, confirm every price, date, stock level and opening hour, delete any discount or claim the AI invented, read each post aloud to test the tone, and approve it yourself. Then paste it in, or use the Channel scheduling tool if your app offers it.

How often to post, and how to measure

Viber publishes no official posting frequency, so this is our judgment: start with one or two useful posts a week, avoid late-night messages, and cut back if followers leave or mute. Each month, open your channel or community info and look at the insights screen, which Viber's help center says is limited to the superadmin. Per Viber's help center, it shows growth, member locations, age, active and new users, messages sent and reactions. It does not show sales, so ask at the counter, "Saan mo kami nakita?" and keep a simple tally.

Honest limits

The free tools give you no ad targeting; you only reach people who joined. Viber's ad formats are separate paid products, and we found no published small-shop pricing. Members can mute you, moderating a Community takes real time, business messaging may need a paid partner, and Viber changes features and policies often.

Frequently asked questions

Is Viber for business free in the Philippines?

Creating a Channel or a Community is free, and Rakuten Viber launched free self-serve Business Accounts for PH MSMEs in October 2023 (BusinessWorld). Official business messaging, such as promotional or transactional messages, is paid and usually goes through an approved partner. We found no published PH price, so ask a provider for a quote.

What is the difference between a Viber Community and a Viber Channel?

A Channel is one-way: only admins post and followers read and react. A Community is two-way: members can chat, phone numbers are hidden and new members see history (Viber help center). Use a Channel for announcements and a Community for regulars who talk back.

How do I get customers to join my Viber channel without spamming them?

Share the invite link or QR code at the counter, on receipts and on your Facebook page, and say what people will get and how often. Do not add people by phone number without asking. The Data Privacy Act (RA 10173) centers on freely given, specific, informed consent and the right to object to direct marketing (privacy.gov.ph). This is general information, not legal advice.

How often should I post on a Viber channel?

Viber does not publish an official frequency, so this is a judgment call. Start with 1-2 useful posts a week, watch member count and reactions in your insights, and cut back if people leave or mute. Skip late-night posts.

What stats does Viber show for my Community or Channel?

Per Viber's help center, the superadmin can open insights to see member growth, member locations, age, active users, new users, messages sent and reactions. It does not show sales, so ask customers where they heard about you.

Can I use AI to write Viber broadcasts?

Yes. Draft short promos, restock notices and booking reminders in English, Tagalog or Taglish with an AI tool, then check every price, date and claim by hand before posting. Paste the text yourself, or use the Channel scheduling tool if your app offers it.
