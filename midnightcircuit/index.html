Two rules from the network that shape everything

Strategy

Selling a casino to someone who came for porn

What the category does

Shouts an offer — "150% FIRST DEPOSIT BONUS" — at a distracted, aroused, goal-directed user, and asks him to switch into financial-decision mode, open a wallet and fill a form. A cold ask at the worst possible moment, so the category compensates with volume and accepts a terrible click-to-deposit rate.

What we do instead

Offer the same feeling he is already chasing, with less friction than the thing he is doing now. No wallet, no form, no decision. One tap, instant result. The money conversation happens later, once he is already in the loop.

The bridge

Slots and porn browsing run on the same reward circuit — variable ratio, anticipation, near-miss, "one more". The user scrolling for the next clip and the user pulling for the next spin are in an identical posture. So don't ask him to switch modes. Hand him the same loop in a different skin. Every unit on this page is one expression of that sentence.

Set G · dirty mind

Nothing Is Happening Here

The best-performing idea in this deck, and the cheapest to defend. It runs on pareidolia — the reflex that makes you read a familiar shape into an abstract one. Every frame below is a photograph of casino furniture and fruit. There is nothing in any of them. The viewer supplies the second reading in about half a second, feels caught out, and looks again — which is the exact attention the placement is bought for. Because the image is provably clean, it survives a creative review that the literal version would not, and it cannot be pulled later.

It is the same engine as GO HARD / WIN BIGGER in Set F, moved from the copy into the picture, and every line is still literally true of the product: a bonus round does open, two of a kind does pay, and it does keep paying.

What we learned getting there, because it decides every future one: the illusion only fires when one symmetrical shape fills the entire frame with nothing competing. A first pass put the objects inside a scene — a lever beside cherries on a slot cabinet, a split peach on a bar — and none of it read, because the eye resolves “slot machine” or “peach on a bar” before it resolves a shape. Shot edge to edge, symmetrical about a vertical axis, on black, all three land. A fourth concept (cupped hands overflowing with coins) was cut: it is a nice picture of hands and produces no double-take at all.

Sizes, QC and what happens next

How these were judged, and what is still to do

Judged at true 1×, on black

Every unit was rendered at actual pixel size on a dark page — never zoomed on a white canvas — at both its resting frame and mid-animation, against a written 14-point bar: brand readable, number reads in under a second, no letter on a busy part of the plate, copy reads top-down, no word twice, no implied winner claim, gold bevel on numbers only, six distinct hooks, three plate-driven layouts, compliance strip present and muted.

Sizes

Everything here is 300×250. The remaining four — 305×99, 300×150, 950×250 and 320×480 — are a re-run of the same templates once the direction is chosen. 320×480 comes first: 84.5% of the audience is on a phone, the deck calls the interstitial "100% attention", and a tall frame is the natural home for a full-height hero.

What drives the traffic

Each unit is a tracked click to Free Games — never a signup wall, never the homepage. Nobody deposits on first contact from this inventory; the site's own free → register → deposit funnel does the rest. Every mechanic × plate gets its own URL so the network's Autopilot and Bidder can rotate spend to the winners. Many hooks, one destination that never asks for money, measurement per hook.

Open items

The 18+ · Play responsibly strip on every unit is a placeholder holding the space; the client confirms which marks must appear. The media kit's contact page is still empty. Final destination URLs and tracking parameters are needed at trafficking. And this is a concept review: none of it is proven until it runs — which is exactly why we ship several variants per slot rather than one hero.
