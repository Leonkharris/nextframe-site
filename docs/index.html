The meta-problem
The company cannot answer its own questions

Two people look for the same thing and get different answers, because it lives in Notion, Google Drive and personal files at once. Proven on the call: a contract was handed to an AI tool to fill in a form and it returned wrong figures. Every bot and agent proposed anywhere in these records reads from that pile. 23 Sep →

Xsino
The player base halved this year

Monthly active players 525 → 259, depositors 227 → 118. It sits in the deck's own facts block and was never discussed. Turnover held up only because the remaining players are heavier — and one account is 18.2% of all wagering. 30 Sep →

Unexamined
14% of deposits reportedly not credited

Attributed to the payment provider in a card about external factors. Mentioned once, picked up by nobody. If it holds it is worth more than every promotion in the deck, and it distorts every deposit-linked conclusion in it. 30 Sep →

Campaign
It cannot report conversions, and a $21 CPM is armed

The network will show zero conversions permanently — no pixel. Twenty channel-coded tracking links exist on our side and nobody has confirmed the client reports against them. Meanwhile a $21.00 CPM bid sits in a paused campaign against a $0.688 minimum. 8 Oct →

One message, unsent
Can the network credit a player's account?

Pay people in the currency they came for — site credit, claimable only through the casino. The only evidence against it is an answer from a general-purpose chatbot. The named contact who could settle it has never been asked. 5 Oct →

Technically trivial. The client's stated reason is to isolate the main brand if an individual store runs into trouble — which changes what the request is. It was answered as a configuration question. 22 Sep →

How these are made

And what to distrust in them

Three guides, six meetings broken down, forty-one pages, nothing left unprocessed. Every recording in the notes folder has been written up.

🎙️
Transcribed on this machine

Audio is pulled with ffmpeg and transcribed locally on the GPU. Nothing is uploaded to a transcription service.

🈯
Mandarin is translated, not transcribed

The model renders Mandarin as English translation, so the sense survives but the exact words do not — and names and numbers come through unreliably. Figures are checked against the screenshots before they are printed.

🖼️
Screens are cropped, then redacted

Screenshots are cropped to the shared content, which removes browser tabs, bookmarks and the participant grid. Player and customer identifiers are covered with solid fills. Commercial figures are left readable — they are the content.

🔒
Not indexed

Every page carries noindex, nofollow and noarchive. These are internal documents on a public host, not public pages.

🏷️

Do not trust the file names. Of the ten recordings in that folder, four were wrong. One meeting exists twice under different names and different dates; a "marketing meeting" is the opening session of the Pornhub campaign; a file called "Morning Meeting, second half" is a different meeting in a different room on an overlapping clock; and the most valuable recording of the set had no name at all beyond its timestamp. Each document says which file it came from and what the name got wrong.

⚖️

Where two write-ups disagreed. The 7 October meeting was written up twice, independently, and the two versions contradicted each other in eighteen places. They have been merged into one, and the contradictions settled by going back to the screenshots rather than by picking a version. Three examples of what that changed:

• The pending-round rows were described as "past seven days and parked". They are 11 to 15 hours old; the seven days is the sports timeout threshold, not the age of those rounds.
• One version said twenty tracking links, the other nineteen. It was twenty (channel IDs 48–67) all along — the screenshot simply cut off the last row. I corrected it to nineteen at one point; the 5 October recording, processed later, shows the allocation in writing as five themes of four links each, which settled it.
• One said the welcome bonus absorbs about half of all live chats. It is 18.9%. The 48.4% figure covers every bonus and promo category together.
