Tokyo Rocks × Chef Todd EnglishTR 05 · The serve
Three ways to spritz
01 · Ice-cold, straight
Open, pour or sip from the can. Best at 38–42°F.
02 · The highball
A tall glass packed with ice, the spritz poured over, a twist of the flavor's own fruit.
03 · The chef's spritz
Over ice, a splash of soda, a fresh garnish: shiso leaf, peach slice, lychee or yuzu peel.
Serving suggestions. Please enjoy responsibly; 21+ only in the United States.

Tokyo Rocks × Chef Todd EnglishTR 08 · Go to market
The route
A proposed four-stage line from launch to global brand. Timing and markets to be agreed with the partner.
Stage 1
Launch
Four flavors, slim cans, the variety pack. Lead market and channels set with the production partner. Chef-led launch events; Todd's restaurants as flagship accounts.
Stage 2
Hospitality
Rooftop bars, beach clubs, hotels and restaurants. An on-premise serve program with the chef's pairings, plus menu and bar placement.
Stage 3
Range
New flavors and seasonal editions, larger formats, and limited collaborations once the core line is established.
Stage 4
Global
International expansion: the Japan edition, markets with strong Japanese-food culture and hospitality partners, under one global brand.
