Tokyo Rocks · Media kitThe brand story
Sake, into a
new social era.
Japanese soul. Tokyo attitude. Todd English taste.
Tokyo Rocks brings sake into a new social era, combining Japanese-inspired flavors, sparkling refreshment and Chef Todd English's culinary creativity.

Inspired by Tokyo's vibrant culture, where tradition meets reinvention, the brand makes sake approachable, modern and ready to enjoy. Four signature flavors define the collection: Yuzu, White Peach, Lychee and Shiso Grapefruit.
Distinctive slim cans and a variety eight-pack (four flavors, two of each) with a carry handle create a striking presence, from retail shelves to restaurant tables, rooftop bars and beach clubs.

With a proposed 5% ABV, Tokyo Rocks is designed to unite culinary character with the convenience of a ready-to-drink spritz. The ambition: a globally recognized sake spritz brand.

Tokyo Rocks · Media kitThe collection
Four flavors.
One point of view.
Y01
Yuzu
Bright by nature.
Bright citrus · Crisp · Refreshing
Serve: Over ice in a tall glass with a twist of yuzu or lemon peel. Pair: Oysters and raw-bar crudo, Tempura and anything fried.
P02
White Peach
A little softer. Just as distinct.
Delicate sweetness · Floral · Elegant
Serve: Over ice with a thin slice of fresh peach. Pair: Spicy tuna on crispy rice, Yakitori with tare glaze.
L03
Lychee
Aromatic. Juicy. Luminous.
Exotic aroma · Juicy · Smooth
Serve: Over crushed ice with a peeled lychee on a pick. Pair: Dim sum and dumplings, Thai and Sichuan heat.
S04
Shiso Grapefruit
An unexpected finish.
Herbal citrus · Dry · Refreshing
Serve: Over ice with a grapefruit wedge and a fresh shiso leaf. Pair: Sushi and sashimi, Herb salads and ceviche.
