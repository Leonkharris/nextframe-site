Tokyo Rocks · Media kitThe story
Sake, into a new social era.
Tokyo Rocks brings sake into a new social era, combining Japanese-inspired flavors, sparkling refreshment and Chef Todd English's culinary creativity.
Inspired by Tokyo's vibrant culture, where tradition meets reinvention, the brand makes sake approachable, modern and ready to enjoy.

Four signature flavors define the collection: Yuzu, White Peach, Lychee and Shiso Grapefruit.
Distinctive slim cans and a variety four-pack with a carry handle create a striking presence, from retail shelves to restaurant tables, rooftop bars and beach clubs.

The ambition: a globally recognized sake spritz brand.

Boilerplate
Tokyo Rocks is a Japanese-inspired sake spritz created with Chef Todd English. Combining Japanese-inspired flavors, sparkling refreshment and Chef Todd English's culinary creativity, it makes sake approachable, modern and ready to enjoy. The collection launches with four signature flavors, Yuzu, White Peach, Lychee and Shiso Grapefruit, in distinctive slim cans and a variety four-pack, at a proposed 5% ABV.

  • A chef's palate in every can, made to sit beside food.
