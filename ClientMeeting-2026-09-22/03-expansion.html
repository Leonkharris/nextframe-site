Needs a legal answer

A separate brand name for every store

The request is technically small and was almost waved through. The reason given for it is what makes it worth stopping on.

0:35:44Client
“On every physical store, or like a different state — can I have my website have a different name for a different state?”
0:36:19Platform
“There is only different brand name, but all the style and the iframe is the same.”Clarified: same system, same domain, different logo, name and link.
0:36:47Client
“When we have customers from a game room or something — if they get busted… the website name on it, nothing happens to our main site. We could just cut that down, right? Easier.”The actual reason.
0:37:07Client
“So every physical store, I want a different name for every physical store. So any physical store that has any problem, nothing can happen to our Best Bet.”
⚖️

This should not be answered by engineering alone. The response on the call was that it is probably easy — logo and name can be changed from the back end — and that it would be discussed internally with the engineers. That is the right answer to the technical question and the wrong place to stop.

The stated purpose is to isolate the main brand from the legal exposure of an individual store. Whether that is ordinary commercial prudence or something that creates liability for the platform is a question for the same legal team that was already engaged on the crypto request below. It should get the same treatment, and a written answer.

Already with legal

A crypto wallet, and operating outside the US

Handled well on the call: the client asked, the platform said yes in principle and no in practice until legal has looked at it.

The request
Own sign-up page and a USDT wallet

The client wants his own registration flow and crypto deposits, citing a competitor that registers players across Bangladesh, Indonesia, Malaysia and Myanmar.

The answer
"Technically it is definitely okay"

But: "We need to be discussed with our legal team as well, because you have already operation in US… in US you are not allowed to use a crypto wallet, but if you are in Malaysia you can use it."

The likely shape
Geo-limiting by IP

"We will limit it for some countries of the IP." So the wallet would exist but not be reachable from the US.

Committed
A written proposal

"We will try to negotiate with our legal team and I will give the answer… we want to be protected for everyone. So we will give you the proposal for this."

Platform side
✅

This is the model for how the branding request should be handled — a clear "the technology is not the constraint, the jurisdiction is", an explicit reason, and a written proposal promised. The branding request got none of that and is the higher-risk of the two.
