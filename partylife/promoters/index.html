Quick answers

Is it free?

Yes. Listing and your profile are free for promoters, organizers, marketers and venues.

How fast will it be listed?

Within a day of getting your details. If something is missing, we message you.

Who sees it?

Anyone on Tonight and the map, plus our Instagram and Facebook audience when we feature it. Only your name, bio and nights are published, with credit. Your contact details never are.

Do I need a poster?

No. A poster is the fastest way, but you can fill in the name, date and venue instead.

Is it open to under-21 nights?

No. Party Life is for 21 and over, so every night we list is too.
