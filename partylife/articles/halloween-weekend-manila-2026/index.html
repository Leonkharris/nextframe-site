Halloween weekend in Manila: every party, prices and door rules

Thirteen parties on Saturday 31 October and Sunday 1 November, from Poblacion and BGC to Intramuros and the bay. 21 and over.

14This weekend3 min read · checked 31 October 2026

Halloween falls on a Saturday this year, so the big nights are tonight. Below is every party we could confirm in a published listing, grouped by area, with the start time, the door price and the door rule where a source gives them. Where two sources disagree, we say so. Prices change at the door, so check the venue's own page before you go.

Poblacion, Saturday 31 October

TRENTAHIN: Home by 12MN at The Fun Roof, 4/F Matheus Building, 5382 General Luna. 8pm, ₱500 with one drink. It is the early one: the party wraps by midnight, so it pairs with a later room.

Madness Manor at Stratosphere, 4/F Century City Mall on Kalayaan. Doors 8:30pm, starts 9pm. ₱1,500 with one drink, or ₱4,800 for four.

Oculto Laboratory at Oculto, T. Tower on P. Burgos. 8pm to 6am, ₱699. The longest night on this list.

Pong District Afterlife at Pong District, 5353 Mariano Street corner General Luna. It also ran Friday. No start time or price published.

BGC, Saturday 31 October

DYSTERA takes over all four rooms of The Palace on 11th Avenue corner 38th Street: Medusa from 6:30pm, Yes Please from 8pm, Clubhouse and Revel from 10pm. No costume, no entry. Pass prices are not on The Palace's site.

The Wicked & The Divine at The Canary, F1 Hotel, 32nd Street corner Lane A. 10pm to 4am. ₱1,429 standing, with two cocktail rounds.

Tea Rave at Huai, Forbes Town. 8pm onwards. No price published.

Makati, Pasig and the bay, Saturday 31 October

SLEEPLESS: The Afterlife at The Eye, Green Sun on Chino Roces, with Firebeatz headlining. 9pm. General admission ₱3,000. VIP is ₱4,000 in one listing and ₱5,000 in another, and the end time is 3am or 4am depending on the source.

Halloween Night at AQUAPLUS, by the Mall of Asia, from the club's own Facebook Page.

Haunted Nights at Vibe Bar's View Deck, Arcovia City, Pasig. 9pm. Walk-in ₱700 with a drink. Tables ₱1,500 a head.

Saturday and Sunday

INTRAMUMU, the Halloween street party on Magallanes Drive in Intramuros, runs both days. ₱550 a day or ₱1,100 for both. The ticket page gives no start time; one listing, citing the organiser's Instagram, says 4pm.

Sunday 1 November

The Upside Down Yacht Party at Manila Bay Yacht Club. Boarding at 6pm. No price published.

AfterLife at AQUAPLUS closes the weekend.

Getting home

Poblacion's four parties are a short walk apart, so a crawl from The Fun Roof at 8pm to Oculto after midnight works on foot. Book your ride home before you leave the last room, especially around Kalayaan and P. Burgos, and around 11th Avenue in BGC when The Palace empties.

Sources, and what we left out

Every party above comes from at least one published listing: What's On Manila, Esquire Philippines, 8List, When In Manila, or the venue's own page. We checked each one on 31 October. We left out parties whose date or venue differs between sources, and parties we found only in a social post we could not open.
