The Promise

Live well.Feel alive.

A place where wellbeing is woven into the pleasures of everyday life — with room for both celebration and personal restoration.

The English helps people feel more alive through experiences they genuinely enjoy. Its distinction is a lively, culturally engaging approach to wellbeing: tranquility remains available, alongside music, shared meals, outdoor adventures, learning and entertainment.

Warm hospitality and beautiful design give guests and residents a reason to return — and a community to belong to.

Where it began

The English Hotel, Las Vegas Arts District.

The English Hotel opened in 2022 on South Main Street, in the Las Vegas Arts District — a 74-room hotel in Marriott’s Tribute Portfolio, built around Chef Todd English’s table.

It proved the idea: that a hotel can be organized around food, a great bar and a sense of community. The English Hotel & Residences takes that idea further — into residences, wellness and culture, across multiple sites.
