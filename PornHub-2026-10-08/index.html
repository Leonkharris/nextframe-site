Before anything launches

Five things to settle first

1 · Measurement
Confirm the channel IDs work

TrafficJunky will report zero conversions forever. Twenty channel-coded tracking links already exist on our side. Confirm the client's system reports registrations and deposits against those channels, and that whoever writes the weekly report knows to pull from there. Detail →

2 · The bids
$21.00 CPM is sitting in a paused campaign

Against a $0.688 minimum and a $19.336 suggestion, on a full-screen popunder. Nobody said who set it or why. Check it before anything is un-paused. Detail →

3 · The floor
Is $25/ad/day really immovable?

Every budget projection depends on it, and Molly's whole testing approach assumes it can be undercut. Ask the account manager directly. Detail →

4 · Success criteria
No target, no threshold, no kill rule

No target CTR, no acceptable cost per click, nothing that triggers "add budget" or "switch it off". Without one, the weekly report describes and does not decide. Detail →

5 · The audience
Asian-American targeting was agreed, then dropped

The platform has no ethnicity targeting. If that is the real audience it has to come through creative, and the twenty creatives built so far are English with Western casting. Detail →

About this document

How it was made, and what was removed

🎙️
Transcribed locally

Both recordings transcribed on this machine, then cross-checked against a frame-by-frame pass of what was on screen. No transcript service was used.

🈯
Mandarin is translated, not transcribed

The model rendered Mandarin stretches as English translation, so exact wording is lost and names and numbers are unreliable. This affects topic 8 heavily and is flagged there.

✂️
Cropped, not blurred

Screenshots are cropped to the shared window only, which removes browser tabs, bookmarks and participant names. The account balance is blurred where it appears.

🔒
Not indexed

Every page carries noindex, nofollow and noarchive. Commercial figures, CPM bids and campaign IDs are left readable because they are the content.

📁

This document contains client commercial terms, live CPM bids and campaign identifiers. It is built to be read and shared internally. It has deliberately not been published to a public URL.
