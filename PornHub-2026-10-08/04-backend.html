The gap, and the half-built bridge

The tracking already exists — it was just never connected to this conversation

This is the most important thing in the whole breakdown, and it was never said out loud in either meeting.

The problem
The client judges on deposits

Budget increases depend on registrations, promotions claimed and second deposits — none of which TrafficJunky can see.

The platform's view
Conversion Tracker sits empty

The campaign's Conversion Tracker already lists Deposit Confirmation Page and Registration Confirmation Page as slots. Both are unfilled because no pixel is being installed.

What we have
20 tracking links, channel-coded

The Midnight Circuit page shared during the call lists all twenty creatives with codes (PH_SAFE_N_P1 onward) and channel IDs 48–67, each with its own tracking link on the client's domain.

The bridge
Attribute on the client's side

If each creative carries its own channel ID into the client's system, then registrations and deposits can be attributed per creative — in the client's reports, not in TrafficJunky's.

🚨

Nobody connected these two facts in either meeting. The TrafficJunky call concluded "you won't see conversions" and moved on. The internal call worried that the client judges on deposits and moved on. Meanwhile twenty channel-coded tracking links were already built and sitting at "Not Live" on our own page.

What needs confirming before launch: that the client's platform actually reports registrations and deposits broken down by those channel IDs, and that whoever writes the weekly report knows to pull them from there rather than from TrafficJunky. If that holds, the measurement problem is solved and the campaign can be judged on real outcomes. If it does not, the campaign will run blind on cost-per-click and the budget conversation will have nothing behind it.
