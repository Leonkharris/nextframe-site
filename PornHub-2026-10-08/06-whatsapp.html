The constraint

There is no "message" objective on this platform

On Meta the team buys a message objective: the ad's job is to open a WhatsApp conversation, and Meta prices and optimises for that. Molly checked during the call and confirmed Pornhub does not offer it.

What Meta gives them

The existing machine

  • Click-to-message as a buyable objective
  • Priced per message, around $3–4
  • respond.io is contracted with Meta, so sender details flow through
  • Works from Facebook, Instagram and direct DMs alike

What TrafficJunky gives them

What they have to build around it

  • No message objective — CPM buying only
  • Priced per thousand impressions, clicks are derived
  • But the ad carries a link, and the link can be anything
  • A wa.me link opens WhatsApp just as well from an adult site
🔗

Juan Carlos settled the point: "As long as there's a link that we can put, it can either go to the page, or go to our WhatsApp, or go to our Messenger. Depending on where we want this click to go." The platform does not need to understand the destination. It only needs to send the click.

Why WhatsApp at all

A click tells you nothing; a message tells you who

This is the argument that made the chatbot worth building rather than just pointing every ad at a landing page.

1

The anonymity problem

Molly: "When a player clicks on the ad you don't really have too much information of this client until you actually register. Before that you don't have anything — you just know that we have a click on this link, but you don't know who clicked the link."

Most clicks never register. On a pure landing-page route, those people are gone and unrecoverable.

2

The WhatsApp route captures a person

The moment the click lands in WhatsApp and the chatbot sends its first message, the conversation exists. The number is captured, the name the person registered on WhatsApp is captured, and the bot pushes the promotion or a registration link automatically.

Juan Carlos summarised it and Molly confirmed: "Even if he doesn't do anything, the casino can always go back and send more message to trigger him." That is the retargeting asset.

The existing Meta machine works exactly this way: ad → WhatsApp → respond.io AI agent → promotion details → register or claim.
🧷

One nuance that was blurred on the call. The reason respond.io can read sender details on Meta traffic is its contract with Meta. That contract does nothing for a click arriving from Pornhub — there, the data capture works simply because WhatsApp itself exposes the sender to the business account. The outcome is the same (number plus profile name), but it arrives by a different route, and anyone writing this up for the client should not claim Meta-grade attribution on Pornhub traffic.

The reason for a second account

Ban risk, and how it was handled

The decision was not about capacity. It was about keeping adult-site traffic away from a WhatsApp number that the business depends on.

The fear
The existing number is tied to Meta

Molly: "The existing one is related to the Meta, so we are very concerned about that — if we connect to Pornhub it will be banned. So we will have another new one."

The decision
A second respond.io agent

A new WhatsApp Business account with its own agent inside respond.io, dedicated to the Pornhub campaign. The Meta-connected account is left untouched.

The cost
No new fee quoted

Asked directly about setup, monthly or maintenance fees. Answer: "No, it will be under the WinFun AI agent right now. It's like he has a second personality."

The timing
Within the week

Work starts Tuesday, normally three to five days to build another one. The call expected it done inside the same week.

⚠️

The ban risk was assumed, not checked. It is a sensible precaution and the cost of taking it is zero, so the decision is right either way. But nobody on the call cited a Meta or WhatsApp Business policy, and nobody confirmed whether a separate account actually insulates the first one — these accounts can be linked by business verification, payment method or portfolio even when the numbers differ. Worth a five-minute check before the campaign goes live, not after.
