Practical reading. A first flight of four creatives at 300x250 is a single campaign that can run across interstitial, footer and middle-banner inventory simultaneously, with per-source reporting underneath so weak zones can be excluded later. That is the cheapest possible way to find out which zone works.

The creative trap

Three CTA overlays sit on top of every pre-roll

This came out of a question about whether our existing layouts would work. For banners, no issue. For pre-roll, the platform draws its own text over the video, and the creative has to leave room for it.

🚫

"For the pre-roll, you see — there's three sections. The first part, this is where it will say there's going to be a text that's automatic. That's from our end… You'll be at the very top right and the bottom right, and then there's going to be at the bottom left where you need to worry about in terms of text overlapping."

Keep the top-right, bottom-right and bottom-left corners of every pre-roll clear of text and logos. Centre and top-left are safe. He confirmed banners have no such problem: "there's nothing that pops up on top of the banner."

File rules

What can be uploaded

Banner campaigns

Display format

  • Max file size 15 MB
  • Static Banner — includes GIFs
  • Video Banner — a 300x250 can be video
  • Banner CTA icon: JPG, PNG only
  • One dimension per campaign, no mixing

Pre-roll campaigns

In-stream video format

  • Max file size 500 MB
  • Audio optional
  • Five guaranteed seconds before skip
  • Three CTA overlay zones to avoid
  • Compliance review: 30–40 minutes
📐

One dimension per campaign. This is a hard rule. "You can't have a 300 by 250 campaign and upload 300 by 100 creatives… you could have multiple creatives that are the same dimension, it's just that you can't overlap a 950 by 250 on a 300 by 250 campaign." You can run as many campaigns as you like on the same dimension — but every size needs its own campaign, and on a $25-per-ad-per-day floor, every extra size has a cost. See the budget page.

3

Static vs video banner is a setting, not a format

On the campaign wizard, Ad Type switches between Static Banner and Video Banner while the dimension stays the same. So a 300x250 slot can carry a short video — at the 15 MB banner limit, not the 500 MB pre-roll limit.

GIFs count as static. If a short looping video is wanted in a banner slot, it has to be compressed to fit inside 15 MB.

Useful because the team already has motion assets built for the Meta campaigns. They can go into banner slots without a re-cut, if they compress.
