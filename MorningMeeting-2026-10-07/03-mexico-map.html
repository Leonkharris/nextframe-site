The problem it solves

"We kind of didn't know where the players were"

JC's framing: the country is roughly fifty times the size of Taiwan, so a promotion aimed at Mexico as a whole is aimed at nothing in particular. Two things needed locating — where players log in, and where the VIPs are.

1

Players by state

A choropleth with a ranked list beside it, filterable by all players or by VIP level 2–5. Mexico City leads on headcount with 2,249, then Jalisco 1,919, Estado de México 1,585, Nuevo León 1,411 and Baja California 1,253.

JC: the team already assumed the north was strongest, "but we didn't know exactly in which states."

2

Deposits by state, which is a different ranking

Headcount and money do not agree. Nuevo León tops deposits at $530,010 MXN from 61 depositors. Nayarit is second at $391,401 from just 11 depositors. Mexico City, first by player count, sits sixth at $164,685.

Of the $2,717,840 total, $2,711,510 came from 720 depositors inside Mexico — the rest is a handful of accounts elsewhere. Coahuila is the one state running negative, at −$22,186 GGR.

The table carries its own warning. A % from top player column shows how much of a state rests on one person, and a without top player column restates the total with that person removed. Nayarit is 99% one player — its $391,401 becomes $5,349 without that single account. Querétaro is 91%. Chiapas is 85%. Sinaloa, San Luis Potosí and Sonora all sit above 79% as well.

The page says it plainly: "Many states depend on a single player: the 'Without top player' column shows the market's real weight."
3

Which providers carry the wagering

Thirty-five providers have play on them, and the top three account for 76% of everything wagered. Pragmatic Play alone is 42.2%: 9,158,487 wagered, 466,950 GGR, 94.9% RTP, 1,582 players, 3.6 million bets. PG Soft is 17.7% and No Limit City 16.2%.

This is why JC asked for refreshed catalogues: "players get excited to try the new slot from Pragmatic, and as you can see 42% of our bets are made in Pragmatic, so it's really important." He asked for the top five providers only, not everything, knowing the team is stretched.

What it is for

Three uses, named in the meeting

Use 1
Local research

For the strongest states, understand what is traditional there, what people like, what they watch. Promotions then speak the local idiom instead of a generic one.

Use 2
Local alliances

Find influencers or YouTubers in states where players already are. JC pointed at the map to show where a YouTuber who used to work with them is based.

Use 3
Recruiting agents

Previously "I need an agent in Mexico" was too broad. JC's example: an agent in Zacatecas is near-useless when almost no players are there.

Worth noticing

What the data quietly says

⚠️
Several states are one person

Nayarit is second by deposits and 99% of it is one player. Treating it as a market would be a mistake; the column exists precisely to stop that.

🎰
Heavy provider concentration

Three providers carry three quarters of wagering. A catalogue or outage problem at Pragmatic Play is not a small event.

💵
Deposits do not follow headcount

Mexico City is first on players and sixth on money. Spend aimed by population would aim wrong.

🛠️
Built on Claude artifacts

Both dashboards are Claude artifacts, not a BI product. Fast to build and easy to share, but they live on individual links.
