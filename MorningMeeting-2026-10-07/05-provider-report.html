The week

RPGaming is the biggest customer by turnover

Mao opened by naming RP as the customer with the highest flow, more than 900 million for the week of 28 September to 4 October. The provider report in the Lucifer aggregator backstage gives the exact figures.

1

The provider report, by game

Almost all of it runs through one provider, GameArt: settled wagering 933,603,291.45 TWD, settled payout 907,393,021.40, settled P&L 26,210,270.05 across 1,658,541 settled rounds.

The grand total across every provider is 933,954,518.13 wagered over 1,685,967 total rounds, so GameArt is 99.96% of the week's wagering. The headline figures above are the all-provider totals; the GameArt row is the line beneath them.

Lunar Rabbit carries most of it: 764.19M wagered against 741.01M paid out. Ragna's Rock is second at 128.45M. Everything after those two drops off sharply — Olympus Majesty and Zeus Blitz Power Strike sit under 10M each.

Note the unsettled column: 3 rounds and 2,197.50 TWD still open at the time of the report. Small in context, and the subject of topic 7.
A complaint, investigated

"Betting 260,000 makes it pay out more"

Players reported that on Lunar Rabbit, staking a particular amount — around 260,000 TWD — made big wins noticeably easier to trigger. Mao had it checked.

✅

Finding: no such problem. The investigation found nothing to support it, and the game's RTP for the week was normal.

Standing instruction: any similar complaint about win rates gets forwarded to GameArt for a formal investigation, as long as the customer can supply data. Mao's phrasing was to help them raise it with the original manufacturer whenever they ask.

Mao also noted the biggest losing game of the week was Ragna's Rock, while Lunar Rabbit and one other were both winning.

He offered to supply per-game RTP from the report whenever a customer queries it, rather than arguing case by case.

The debt

September's unsettled and suspended rounds

This is where the meeting turns technical, and it sets up topic 7. In September the account carried unsettled orders and suspended rounds. Mao wanted both summarised and a process put around them.

The pattern
The problem originates with GameArt

Lucifer sits in the middle as the aggregator and service provider, so the operator sees Lucifer's name on a problem the game provider caused.

The fix
Stop waiting to be told

"Don't wait until RP discovers it and then we deal with it." From October, monitor unsettled and suspended rounds directly.

The mechanism
Surface anything stuck past 24 hours

Pull those rounds out, ask the provider, and if the provider's list does not contain them, settle manually.

The complication
