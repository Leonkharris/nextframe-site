The idea

Why chase old VIPs instead of new players

JC gave the number that justifies the whole exercise: research on previously reactivated VIPs suggests each one is worth roughly a hundred new members. With about 78 VIPs currently asleep, the target is to wake at least 10% of them.

🎯

"Our goal is to at least wake up 10% of them. Our research has shown that from one of these VIPs that we are able to wake up, it's around having 100 new members. So it's really important for us to get them up again."

October, as the board saw it

At the time of the meeting every counter read zero. Deposits reported for the month stood at $0 against a target of $319,300 MXN, derived as $10,300 a day across 31 days, with pace shown as 0%.

The daily quotas above it read 0 of 18 contacts, 0 of 6 replies, $0 of $10,300 in deposits and 0 of 2 reactivated this week, each labelled Behind. Segment counters were likewise at zero: C depositing 0/9, D recovered 0/3, E reactivated 0/8, A with first deposit 0/3, contacts this month 0/558, B with risk review 0/7.

JC said directly that the board was not yet current: "this hasn't been updated with what finished today." The special VIP promotion behind it launched the previous Thursday, so the zeroes reflect a reporting lag rather than a dead programme.

What the team records

Each row carries segment, account, level, status, number of deposits, amount deposited, net, days, channel, last contact and a notes field. Tabs across the top split it into today's queue, all accounts, a contact log and the rules. The notes are working CSR observations, written in Spanish, recording things like a player having blocked the casino's number or only responding on one channel.
