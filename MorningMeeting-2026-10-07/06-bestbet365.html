The week

Turnover keeps climbing

Mao reported the US version's best result since it opened: around 730,000 USD of turnover in the week, and still rising. More customers and more volume are bringing more requests with them.

1

The weekly P&L board

The console's Figures page covers profit and loss, player analysis and agent performance. For 28 September to 4 October, the master agent MasterBet365 ran from a 393.98 day through a −5,987.47 day to a +12,813.03 day, finishing the period at +20,515.82.

Across 20 players the customer total came to +USD 20,737.56 for the period. Two other master agents are listed, PromotionOff at −1.78 and VinceMasterAgent at +223.02.

Day-to-day swings are large relative to the weekly total. One bad Friday wiped out more than the whole week's eventual profit before the weekend recovered it.
2

The wager ledger, and what it cannot yet show

Wager records list every bet with stake, payout and status. The filtered subtotal on screen reads 1,273,013.56 staked against 1,162,546.79 paid out, a difference of 110,466.77, across 14,572 results.

The rows are NBA bets placed in the early hours of 7 October — Utah Jazz versus Denver Nuggets among them — with stakes from 88.00 to 2,000.00 USD.

Mao's point: for unsettled bets you can tell which team it is, but the detail is not there. "The score is still not out." That is the gap to close.
