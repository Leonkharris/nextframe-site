The decision

Deposit first, then withdraw

The welcome bonus has been given without requiring a deposit. That is changing in the next few days: to withdraw, the player must deposit.

↩️

This is a reversal, not an invention. The deposit requirement existed when Xsino launched and was removed. Majo: "at the beginning when we started Xsino, it was one rule, but we took it out. Right now I think we have been too long without deposit, so we will add deposit again and see how it goes."

Why now

Two reasons were given, one commercial and one operational.

Commercial
Conversion is low and falling

The gap between players requesting the bonus and players going on to deposit is "too low and it's getting lower". The read: the offer attracts people who only ever intend to play free.

Operational
It eats the support team

Bonus questions dominate live chat, and the verification queue behind them is largely the same traffic. "We're investing a lot of time, and time is money, on the welcome bonus."

📊

The live-chat evidence

JC's analysis classifies every 2026 chat by what the player actually writes. Across 14,635 chats from 6,999 unique visitors, bonuses dominate.

Welcome bonus is the single largest topic at 18.9%. Codes and promos add 18.6%, and verification, which Majo notes is usually in service of the welcome bonus, adds 18.4%. The platform's own summary tile puts bonus and promo chats at 48.4% of all conversations.

The underlying counts, from the same screen: 14,635 chats classified across 1 January – 30 September, from 6,999 unique visitors, with 1,659 accounts identified. Welcome bonus 2,762 · codes and promos 2,729 · verification (KYC/SMS) 2,698 · other bonuses 1,267 · deposits and top-ups 1,188. The 48.4% summary tile covers every bonus and promo category together, not the welcome bonus alone — a distinction worth holding on to, because a second write-up of this meeting read that tile as "the welcome bonus absorbs about half of all chats", which is not what it says.
Majo's shorthand in the meeting was "almost 40% of the chats are related to welcome bonus". The chart supports that once codes and promos are counted with it.
What replaces it

Three additions, one of them still a question

✅ Adding
Mancala free spins

Run through Mancala, which the team already uses for promotion mechanics.

✅ Adding
A valid-bet tournament

Set a threshold of valid bets; when a member crosses it, a prize fires. Majo compares it to a mechanic Gili ran. Planned for Saturdays.

🔧 Proposed
A second-deposit bonus

There is a first-deposit bonus today and nothing after it. Majo wants one for the second deposit, with the caveat: "we'll have to check that out, write it down, and see if it's possible to do it on the system."

🤖

How these were chosen. Majo credits generated data cross-referenced by AI: "we have created, generated a lot of data and the data has been crossed by AI, and from that we have created some reports focusing on which promotions we can change, which days, and which ones to take out."
