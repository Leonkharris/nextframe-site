A reason to sign up now

The ad offers something the visitor can only get by registering, so the form is the way to the reward, not a chore before it.

A smaller ask

Trying a game on free demo credits needs no deposit, so “register to play free” asks less of a first-time visitor than “deposit to play”.

One message, start to finish

Ad, landing page and button say the same thing, which cuts the drop-off between the click and the form.

Every free player is an account

Open demo play lets visitors leave anonymous. Gated play turns them into registered players you can invite back to deposit, with their consent.

We do not assume a lift figure. Weeks 2–4 measure it: C1–C10 run in small, balanced creative cells on the same placements, and landing-to-registration % is compared side by side.

05The share is settled

GGR on these games is shared between 247iBET and us at the agreed rate, on the agreed cycle.

What GGR means here

Gross gaming revenue = real-money bets − real-money wins on the 9game titles played on 247iBET through the integration. Free-play rounds create no GGR. Everything else your new players deposit and play elsewhere on 247iBET stays outside this share.
