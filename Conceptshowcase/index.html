About Todd

Four James Beard Awards · 50+ restaurants · Eight countries

Todd English started cooking at fifteen. He graduated with honors from the Culinary Institute of America, trained under Jean-Jacques Rachou at La Côte Basque, then left for Italy and came back with an idea that changed American dining: rustic Mediterranean food, cooked with fire and conviction. Olives opened in Charlestown in 1989 with fifty seats and a wood oven; within a decade it was a global name.

Four James Beard Awards followed — along with television, cookbooks, restaurants in eight countries, and a generation of cooks who came up through his kitchens. Today he runs the plays he invented through The English Hospitality Group: from Walt Disney World to Bali, a 24/7 food hall in Abu Dhabi, and a residents-only concept rising sixty-two stories above Miami.

Chef Todd English · A World of Dining Concepts01 · Tokyo Rocks Food Hall
The Concept

Tokyo after dark, served all day.

Tokyo Rocks Food Hall brings the energy of Tokyo's yokocho alleys into one great room. A ring of chef-led counters — ramen, robata, hand rolls, katsu sandos, soft serve — surrounds a central sake bar pouring Tokyo Rocks Sake Spritz.

Quick to order, easy to linger: lunch for the office crowd, dinner for the city, late nights for everyone else. Every counter carries Chef Todd English's recipes; the hall carries one name.

Give us the space— here is what we make of it
The spaceA large open floor — a converted warehouse, a podium level or a ground-floor hall with street presence.
What we buildEight to ten open-kitchen counters, a central sake & highball bar, long communal tables in pale timber.
The experienceOrder at any counter, eat together. Steam, charcoal, lanterns and music from noon to late.
Who it bringsOffice lunch, groups and families, late-night locals, visitors looking for Tokyo.

The Concept

Hidden behind velvet. Found by the glow.

Jade is an intimate supper club and late-night lounge blending refined Asian influences with modern luxury — inspired by Tokyo's after-hours culture, private supper clubs and old-world glamour.

Carved beneath vaulted arches and wrapped in deep emerald velvet, it feels subterranean, intentional and impossibly private. The music doesn't dominate; it envelops.

Give us the space— here is what we make of it
The spaceAn intimate room with a sense of discovery — a lower level, a hidden entrance, the hotel's after-dark address.
What we buildVaulted emerald velvet lounge, a backlit bar of rare spirits, low curved booths and a performance podium.
The experienceSupper first, then the night set: signature cocktails, premium sake, caviar service, a live jazz trio or guest DJ.
Who it bringsThe hungry, the curious, and the ones who stay for one more.

The Concept

Eat. Real. Food.

The Blue Kitchen is premium fast-casual Mediterranean from Chef Todd English — healthy, tasty, with a touch of Greek flavor. Signature bowls, wraps and small plates built on charcoal-grilled proteins, ancient grains and fresh market vegetables, made fresh every day.

Bowls land at 500–700 calories with up to 48g of protein: the speed of a counter, the cooking of a chef.

Give us the space— here is what we make of it
The spaceA storefront in a mall, transit hub, office lobby or food hall — anywhere guests need real food, fast.
What we buildA navy zellige ordering counter with a four-step menu board, an open charcoal kitchen, grab-and-go, and a bright dining room on blue-and-white tile.
The experienceChoose a base, a protein, Mediterranean sides and a signature finish — made in front of you, eaten in or taken to go.
Who it bringsTravelers, shoppers, the office lunch crowd, fitness regulars and families.
