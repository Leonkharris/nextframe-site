Concentration

One player is 18% of the business

The spike question had a dull answer on the call — "a big win from a VIP". The deck is far more specific.

The individual
18.2% of all wagering

3.91M staked by one account, generating 28.8% of GGR. Name redacted in the screenshot above.

The record day
89% of it was him

Of the 15 highest-wagering days, 10 had a single player at half or more. On 9 of those it was the same person. The record — 11 September, 562k — was 89% his.

The top 20
62% of wagering

Top 1% of players is 20 people. Top 5% (102 players) is 87%; top 10% is 92%.

The winners
35 players took 1.85M

198 separate wins of 5,000 or more, concentrated in 35 accounts.

🎯

Read the daily chart accordingly. The deck colours days where one player was half or more of the volume — so the line is not a trend with spikes on it, it is a base plus a handful of individuals. Reporting GGR without that split invites reading a quiet month from a whale as a business decline, which is exactly what recommendation 5 below asks for.

🎰

The same shape at game level. Dragon Hatch 2 is the biggest game by stake at 1.44M — from 12 players. Fire in the Hole 3 takes 951k from 27. By contrast Fortune of Olympus turns over 306k across 266 players. A "top game" here can mean one whale's habit.

Promotions

What the bonuses actually do

Two promotions were singled out, and four are losing money outright.

1

The Wednesday Telegram code brings players, not deposits

Code #116 is almost perfectly scheduled: 97.7% of its claims land on a Wednesday, and 699 of 876 fall between 18:00 and 18:59. That is the entire reason Wednesday is the strongest day in the heatmap.

The recommendation is blunt — it brings people in without bringing money in. Tie it to a minimum deposit, or move it to Saturday, when people do deposit.

2

The Thursday 50% is loaded by hand, and mostly cancelled

Code #086 has no code in the admin at all. 120 of the 210 manual bonuses issued this year fall on Thursdays, each exactly 50% of a deposit made in the prior 24 hours with a 20× rollover.

It was used by 48 players across 32 Thursdays. 103 claims were cancelled and only 6 completed. It costs 39,788 in bonus and does not move Thursday's total.

The ask is specific: give it a real code in the admin and test it against a control group before expanding it.
💸

Four codes are net negative. #063 Adivina el símbolo at −45,105 is an order of magnitude worse than the rest — #127 −4,777, #071 −6,424, #087 −1,665. Nobody raised these on the call. The first one alone is roughly the cost of the entire Thursday 50% programme.

The deck's own conclusions

Six recommendations, in the order given

1
Protect the VIPs who drive the spikes

Assigned host, reviewed responsible-gaming limits, and an alert if they stop playing for three days.

2
Scale the deposit streak, fix the Thursday 50%

#131 lifts deposits with a cheaper bonus; #086 costs more and moves nothing. Give #086 a code and a control group.

3
Redesign the Wednesday Telegram code

Tie #116 to a minimum deposit, or move it to Saturday.

4
Shift budget to weekends and holidays

Where deposits and depositors rise consistently.

5
Separate luck from trend in reports

Report GGR alongside the month's wins over 5,000, so a winning streak is not read as decline.

6
Start recording what isn't stored

Withdrawal rejection reasons, affiliate campaign dates and channels, RTP and game changes. Without them, external hypotheses cannot be tested at all.

Data integrity

Three things found while looking

Payments
14% of deposits are not credited

Attributed to the payment provider Starpago in the "external factors" card. If it holds, it dwarfs every promotion on this page — and it was not discussed.

Missing data
18 May has no bets at all

Not a quiet day — no records on the server, confirmed directly against the API. An entire day is absent from every figure here.

Inconsistency
The tabs disagree on GGR

The summary says 1,004,661; the betting tab says 986k at a 4.6% margin. Close, but not the same number. Pick one before either is quoted externally.

🔍

The Starpago line deserves its own meeting. Fourteen per cent of deposits failing to credit is a revenue leak and a support-ticket generator, and it would also distort every deposit-linked conclusion on this page. It appears once, inside a card about external factors, and nobody picked it up.
